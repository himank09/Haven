      Images

      Turn on Image in the chat box, or just ask "draw…", to make a picture. Pick the model next to the Image button; Automatic uses the free ones first.

      Google Gemini (Nano Banana)

      Uses your Gemini key from Models & keys. Google only makes images for keys with billing turned on (about $0.04 each).

      Hugging Face

      FLUX.1 schnell on Hugging Face's free Space: free, with a daily allowance of shared GPU time (a token gives your account's allowance). Stable Diffusion 3 through Inference Providers needs credits or PRO. Create a token with "Make calls to Inference Providers" at huggingface.co/settings/tokens; a Hugging Face key under Models & keys works too.

      Cloudflare Workers AI Free

      FLUX.1-schnell with a daily free allowance. In the Cloudflare dashboard open AI → Workers AI, copy your Account ID and create a "Workers AI" API token.

      Pollinations Free

      No account or key at all. Your description goes to pollinations.ai, a small free service with no privacy promises, so the Privacy Shield still hides personal details first.
